The right order matters

Traffic is only useful if your next step is ready.

This offer is built for service-based businesses that can take inquiries now—not for sending paid clicks to a page with no clear way to contact you.

Who it's for

Service businesses ready to take the next lead.

  • You provide a service and want customers in a defined area to contact you.
  • You have a functioning site or lead page with a clear call, booking, or inquiry path—or are open to building one separately.

  • You agree on the scope, budget, and measurement plan before launch.

Not every business or website will qualify. We will review fit before offering complimentary work.

Clear terms up front

What happens after the two months?

If you continue
Ongoing management starts at $325 per month, subject to the agreed scope. Your Google ad spend remains separate.
If you don't continue
We pause the managed campaign for up to 50 days in case you choose to restart under our management. There is no automatic management charge merely for applying.
Your Google Ads account
Each advertiser has a separate Google Ads account. You retain your account access and data; we do not take those away. Our proprietary planning process and templates are not included as a handoff in the complimentary offer.
Before we begin
We'll confirm eligibility, daily spend, dates, the services included, and any additional website work in a written agreement. Ads do not guarantee a specific number of leads or customers.
