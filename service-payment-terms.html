1. Scope of services and agreements

Colonna Media LLC provides photography and headshots, videography and video editing, photo editing, social media setup, content production and management, Google Ads and Meta Ads setup and management, analytics setup and reporting, website design, maintenance and hosting, and strategy and consulting services.

The applicable signed service agreement, proposal, or statement of work defines the services purchased, deliverables, pricing, schedule, and any minimum commitment. These general terms supplement those documents. If these terms conflict with the applicable signed service agreement, that signed agreement controls for the engagement. A payment does not, by itself, mean a separate agreement has been signed.

2. Pricing and payment

The amount, billing frequency, and any upfront payment or deposit are disclosed in the applicable agreement, invoice, or checkout. Additional services, travel expenses, extra revisions, and work outside the agreed scope require your approval before we bill for them. Applicable taxes may be added where legally required.

3. One-time services and deposits

One-time services are charged according to the agreed payment schedule. Any deposit requirement and the conditions for a deposit refund are disclosed in the applicable agreement.

4. Recurring billing authorization

For recurring services, you authorize Colonna Media LLC, through its payment processor, to charge your selected payment method at the amount and frequency displayed at checkout and described in the applicable agreement. Recurring charges continue until cancellation takes effect under that agreement.

Any initial term, renewal conditions, cancellation deadline, or early termination obligation must be clearly disclosed before purchase in the applicable agreement.

5. Cancellation of recurring services

You may request cancellation by emailing colonnamedia@gmail.com or using another cancellation method specifically provided for your service. The applicable agreement governs when cancellation takes effect and whether any outstanding charges remain due. Simply stopping use of a service does not submit a cancellation request. Nothing here limits cancellation rights provided by law.

6. Refunds

Payments for completed work and services already delivered are generally nonrefundable, except where the applicable agreement or law provides otherwise. For prepaid work that has not been completed, deposits, or unused service periods, refund eligibility is governed by the applicable agreement and applicable law. These terms do not limit rights that cannot legally be waived.

7. Appointments, rescheduling, and missed sessions

Photography, videography, and consulting appointments may have cancellation deadlines, rescheduling fees, or missed-session charges only where disclosed in the applicable agreement before booking. Weather, illness, access issues, and other disruptions will be handled according to the applicable agreement and reasonable coordination between us.

8. Client responsibilities

Please provide timely access, approvals, accurate information, and the materials needed for us to perform the work. You are responsible for having permission to use any materials you provide. Client delays may affect delivery dates; any resulting changes in scope or charges require agreement.

9. Photography and video delivery

Photos and videos are generally delivered through an online folder for you to download. The applicable agreement defines the deliverables, editing, revisions, usage rights, and any delivery or download-access period. Raw files, storage beyond an agreed period, and any transfer of copyright depend on the applicable agreement. Portfolio or promotional use requires separate authorization or a provision in the applicable agreement.

10. Advertising and social media services

Advertising management fees are separate from photography, videography, and other content production fees unless expressly included. Advertising platform spend and third-party charges are also separate unless expressly included.

Results depend on factors outside our control. We do not guarantee a particular number of leads, sales, followers, rankings, or return on ad spend. You remain responsible for approving factual claims, offers, and materials about your business.

11. Website services and hosting

Website ownership, licensing, hosting, domain access, maintenance, transfers, and termination arrangements depend on the applicable signed agreement. Some arrangements involve Colonna Media retaining ownership and hosting the website. Review your specific agreement for the arrangements that apply to your site.

12. Third-party services

Payment processors such as Stripe, advertising platforms, hosting providers, and other third parties have their own terms and may experience outages or make changes. Using a payment processor does not, by itself, handle tax filing, legal compliance, or refunds for either party.

13. Service issues and disputes

Please contact colonnamedia@gmail.com promptly with billing questions or service concerns so we can review them and attempt to resolve them. This does not restrict your right to make a lawful payment dispute or chargeback.

14. Changes to these terms

These terms are effective November 16, 2026, version 1.0. Updates apply prospectively, subject to existing signed agreements and any applicable notice or consent requirements. Posting an update does not automatically change an existing contract or authorize new charges.
