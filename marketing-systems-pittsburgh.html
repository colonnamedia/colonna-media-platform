Where does the customer journey break?

You may have social media content, but no useful website destination. Your ads may be running, but the landing page never asks visitors to take action. Or you may have an email tool with no working signup or inquiry follow-up. Improving one channel without checking the next step can leave the same problem in place.

Anthony looks across your website, local presence, social accounts, advertising, lead capture, email, and measurement. Then we prioritize the missing pieces according to the business goal, not a prepackaged list of tactics.
